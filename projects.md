## LLM Tool Call Injections

A collection of documented tool-call injection payloads targeting AI coding assistants and LLM-integrated applications. Catalogs techniques for injecting malicious tool invocations through in-band signaling across multiple LLM providers.

Includes the Deepseek DSML injection payload from our [published Kiro IDE vulnerability research](/blog/2026/09/01/kiro-deepseek-dsml-injection/), along with defense patterns and architectural mitigations.

**[github.com/hackerbuddy/llm-tool-injections](https://github.com/hackerbuddy/llm-tool-injections)**

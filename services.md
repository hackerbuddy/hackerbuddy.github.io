# Services

ArmorKeeper provides hands-on security services for organizations that need more than automated scan reports and checkbox compliance. Every engagement is scoped to the problem and delivered by someone who has built and defended the same kinds of systems you run.

---

## Security Assessments

Manual, targeted testing of your systems by someone who understands both how to break them and how to build them correctly. We go beyond scanner output and deliver findings your engineering team can actually act on.

**What we test:**
- Web applications and APIs
- Cloud environments (AWS, Azure, GCP) — including infrastructure-as-code review
- Mobile applications
- IoT and embedded systems
- AI and LLM integrations — prompt injection, model manipulation, data leakage, and tool-calling exploits

---

## Compliance & Audit Readiness

Getting audit-ready shouldn't mean six months of panic. We guide you through the entire process — gap assessment, policy development, control implementation, and evidence collection — so you're prepared before the auditor arrives, not scrambling during the audit.

**Frameworks we work with:**
- SOC 2 Type I and Type II
- PCI DSS
- General security program maturity assessments

---

## Training & Workshops

Practical, hands-on training for your engineering and operations teams. In-person or remote, built around real vulnerabilities and real-world scenarios — not slide decks.

**Topics include:**
- Secure coding practices for your tech stack
- AI security awareness — safe adoption of AI tools, prompt injection risks, securing LLM integrations
- Threat modeling for development teams
- Cloud security fundamentals for developers and ops

---

## Virtual CISO

For organizations that need security leadership but aren't ready for a full-time hire. As your vCISO, we provide ongoing strategic guidance, own your security program, and serve as your point of contact for security decisions.

**What a vCISO engagement looks like:**
- Regular security program reviews and roadmap planning
- Architecture and design review for new products and features
- Vendor risk management and security questionnaire support
- Incident response planning and coordination
- Board and leadership reporting on security posture
- Compliance oversight and audit liaison

This is a monthly retainer engagement, scoped to your needs. Most clients start with an assessment or training engagement and grow into an ongoing advisory relationship.

# About ArmorKeeper

ArmorKeeper is a security consultancy focused on hands-on assessments, developer training, and security program advisory for small and mid-size companies.

## Background

ArmorKeeper is built on 10 years of experience across both sides of the security problem — building secure systems and breaking insecure ones. That means software engineering, secure code development, developer training, and security operations, not just penetration testing.

This is a purple team practice. We don't just hand you a list of findings and walk away. We understand how your engineering team builds, how your operations team defends, and where the gaps between them create real risk. That perspective comes from having done both jobs.

Our research background includes contributions through major bug bounty and vulnerability disclosure programs, including published security research against production AI development tools. We bring that attacker mindset to every engagement — but pair it with the builder's understanding of what it takes to actually fix things.

## Approach

- **Purple team mindset.** We attack and defend. Findings come with context on how to fix them, not just proof they exist.
- **Built for builders.** Reports are written for engineers, not compliance auditors. If your developers can't act on the findings, the engagement failed.
- **In person when it matters.** On-site demos, trainings, and presentations are available for engagements where face time makes the difference.
